Privacy Policy for Vestigia

Last updated: December 30, 2024

Vestigia ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application uses your information.

1. Camera Usage

Our app requires access to your device's camera to identify historical landmarks. Images are processed purely for analysis and are not permanently stored on our servers or shared with third parties for marketing.

2. Location Data

We use your location data to show nearby historical places on the map and to improve the accuracy of the AI analysis. Location data is used only while the app is in use.

3. Data Deletion

Users can request the deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us or using the "Delete Account" option within the app profile settings.
